          Paid the wrong account?
          1. Call your bank now, on the number on your card, and ask them to recall or stop the transfer. Speed matters most.
          2. Tell your family, and call the university finance office on the number on its own website, not one from a message.
          3. Keep everything: the bill, the messages, the receipt and the sender's details. Do not delete the chat.
          4. Report it to the police where you live, and in Bangladesh to the bank that sent the money.
          5. Do not pay anyone who offers to get the money back for a fee. That is a second scam.
